# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

OPlates welcomes respectful contributions and discussions from everyone. Help keep the project useful, focused and friendly.

Positive behavior includes:

- being respectful and patient;
- giving clear, constructive technical feedback;
- assuming good intent while discussing evidence;
- respecting different experience levels and backgrounds;
- keeping reports focused on the project;
- accepting maintainers’ decisions about project scope.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, insults or discriminatory language;
- sexualized content or unwanted attention;
- publishing private information without permission;
- spam, repeated off-topic demands or deliberate disruption;
- impersonating the project, author or another contributor;
- using project spaces to distribute malware, cheats or stolen material.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests or other contributions that violate this standard. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to **FreakySphere** through the official GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible. Do not include account passwords, authentication tokens or unrelated private information.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the OPlates repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.
