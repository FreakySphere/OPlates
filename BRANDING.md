# OPlates Branding and Asset Policy

This policy explains which OPlates materials are **not** granted under the GNU GPL that covers the addon source code. It is intended to protect the identity of the official project without restricting the GPL rights in the code.

Copyright © 2026 FreakySphere. All rights reserved in the protected materials identified below.

## Protected identity

The following identify the official project and are reserved to FreakySphere except for the limited uses expressly allowed by this policy:

- the **OPlates** name when used to identify an official or endorsed release;
- the author identity **FreakySphere**;
- the OPlates Logo and converted or derivative forms of it;
- official repository and support links when used to imply origin or endorsement;
- original Support OPlates prose, signature, QR code and dedicated Support artwork.

Relevant protected asset files include:

- `OPlates/Art/Logo.blp`;
- `docs/images/logo.png`;
- `docs/images/usdt-trc20-qr.png`;
- `OPlates/Art/Support.tga`;
- `OPlates/Art/SupportQR.tga`.

The source code in `OPlates/UI/Support.lua`, like the other original OPlates source code, is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later. This policy does not restrict the GPL right to modify that code. It does restrict false claims that a modified build is official and unauthorized reuse of separately protected visual/literary branding.

## Allowed uses

You may:

1. use the name OPlates truthfully to refer to this project;
2. link to the official repository;
3. include protected materials unchanged when redistributing an **unmodified official OPlates release**, with clear credit to FreakySphere and a link to the official repository;
4. use small, unmodified Logo reproductions in reviews, documentation, news, compatibility lists or other nominative references to OPlates; and
5. state that a fork is “based on OPlates” when the statement is accurate and does not imply endorsement.

## Modified versions and forks

GPL-licensed code may be modified and redistributed under GPL-3.0-or-later. A modified version must also comply with the GPL requirement to identify changes.

Unless FreakySphere gives written permission, a modified public distribution must:

- clearly state that it is **unofficial** and modified;
- identify its maintainer separately from FreakySphere;
- avoid implying approval, sponsorship or endorsement by FreakySphere;
- use a distinct project name and visual identity if its presentation could reasonably be confused with the official OPlates release;
- replace protected Logo and Support visual assets when distinct branding is required; and
- preserve truthful copyright and authorship notices for original work.

These requirements concern branding, attribution and protected non-code assets. They do not remove or narrow rights granted by GPL-3.0-or-later in the source code.

## Prohibited uses

Without written permission, you may not:

- claim a modified build is an official OPlates release;
- claim that FreakySphere created, reviewed or endorsed changes that FreakySphere did not create, review or endorse;
- remove FreakySphere’s truthful authorship attribution from original OPlates work;
- use the Logo or Support visual assets as the identity of a confusingly similar fork;
- alter the Logo and present the result as official OPlates branding;
- redirect official donation or repository links while presenting a build as official; or
- use OPlates identity or protected assets for malware, credential theft, cheating automation or fraud.

## Other artwork

Original OPlates artwork not listed as protected above is distributed with the project under GPL-3.0-or-later and may be corrected or improved under that license. Third-party assets, if any, retain their own terms.
